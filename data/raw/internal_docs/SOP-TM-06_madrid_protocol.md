# SOP-TM-06 - Madrid Protocol Filings (Outgoing and Incoming)
## 1. Outgoing International Applications
- Indonesian clients may file an international application through DJKI as the Office of origin, based on an Indonesian application or registration.
- Internal timeline: the draft MM2 form is prepared within **3 working days** of receiving complete instructions.
- Professional fee: **IDR 5,000,000** per international application, plus **IDR 750,000** per designated country. WIPO fees are passed through at cost.
- The associate must remind the client that, for **5 years** from the international registration date, the international registration depends on the basic Indonesian mark (central attack).

## 2. Incoming Designations of Indonesia
- When the firm is appointed by a foreign firm to handle a provisional refusal for an international registration designating Indonesia, the response deadline is diarised immediately.
- Communication with the foreign firm is in **English**. Reports must be sent within **2 working days** of receiving any DJKI notice.

## 3. Renewal of International Registrations
International registrations are renewed with WIPO every **10 years**. The docketing team sends reminders **6 months** before the WIPO renewal date.
