# SOP-TM-04 - Trademark Opposition (Keberatan) Procedure
## 1. Filing an Opposition for a Client
- Oppositions must be filed during the **2-month publication period**.
- The watch service team sends a watch notice to the client within **3 working days** of spotting a potentially conflicting publication.
- Client instructions must be received at least **10 working days** before the end of the publication period. Later instructions are treated as urgent.
- Required evidence: proof of prior rights, evidence of use or reputation, and the signed Power of Attorney.
- Professional fee: **IDR 6,500,000** per opposition, excluding official fees and translation costs.

## 2. Defending Against an Opposition
- When a client's application is opposed, the applicant may submit a counter-statement (sanggahan). The firm's internal target is to deliver the draft counter-statement to the client within **10 working days** of receiving the opposition copy.
- Professional fee for a counter-statement: **IDR 5,000,000**.

## 3. Quality Control
- Every opposition brief is reviewed by a partner before filing.
- Outcomes (successful, partially successful, rejected) are logged in the Opposition Tracker so the firm can report its success rate quarterly.
