# SOP-TM-05 - Trademark Renewal and Docketing
## 1. Background
An Indonesian trademark registration lasts **10 years** and may be renewed for the same period. A renewal may be filed within **6 months before** the expiry date, and late renewal is possible up to **6 months after** expiry with an additional official fine.

## 2. Reminder Schedule (client reminders)
The docketing system sends renewal reminders to the client at **12 months, 6 months, 3 months and 1 month** before expiry. After expiry, a final grace-period reminder is sent every **30 days** until the grace period ends.

## 3. Procedure
1. The docketing team verifies the registration details and the current owner name.
2. If the owner name or address has changed, a recordal of change is filed **before or together with** the renewal.
3. The associate files the renewal and reports the filing receipt within **1 working day**.

## 4. Fees
- Renewal professional fee: **IDR 2,500,000** per class.
- Late renewal (grace period) professional fee: **IDR 3,500,000** per class.

## 5. Lapsed Registrations
If the client gives no instruction by the end of the grace period, the matter is closed with status **TM-LAPSED** and the client receives a closing letter.
