# POL-CONF-01 - Confidentiality and Data Protection Policy
## 1. Principle
All client information, including unpublished applications, inventions and business plans, is confidential.

## 2. Rules for Staff
- Client documents may only be stored in the firm's document management system, never on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.
- Unpublished patent specifications must be sent to clients and translators only through encrypted links that expire after **7 days**.
- Staff must not enter client confidential information into public AI tools. Approved internal AI tools may be used only if listed in the IT Approved Tools Register.

## 3. Personal Data
The firm processes personal data in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP). Personal data requests from individuals are handled by the Data Protection Officer within **14 days**.

## 4. Incidents
Any suspected data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within **24 hours** of discovery. The DPO decides whether clients and authorities must be notified.

## 5. Retention
Closed matter files are retained for **10 years** after closure and then securely destroyed.
