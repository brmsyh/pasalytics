# POL-BILL-02 - Payment Terms and Collection
## 1. Payment Terms
- Standard payment term: **30 days** from the invoice date.
- New clients with no payment history pay official fees **in advance** for any filing.

## 2. Collection Steps for Overdue Invoices
1. Day 31: friendly reminder email from Finance.
2. Day 45: second reminder copied to the responsible associate.
3. Day 60: the responsible partner contacts the client directly.
4. Day 90: new non-urgent work is put on hold, with written notice to the client. Deadline-critical work is never stopped without partner approval.

## 3. Write-offs
Write-offs up to **IDR 5,000,000** may be approved by the responsible partner. Larger write-offs require approval from the **Managing Partner**.

## 4. Disputes
Clients who dispute an invoice must be answered within **5 working days**. The disputed amount is excluded from the overdue count while under review.
