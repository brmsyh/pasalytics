# SOP-TM-02 - Domestic Trademark Filing
## 1. Scope
Filing new trademark applications with the Directorate General of Intellectual Property (DJKI) for Indonesian and foreign clients.

## 2. Documents Required From the Client
- Signed Power of Attorney (see POL-KYC-01). For foreign clients a simple signed copy is accepted at filing; no legalisation is required by firm policy.
- Mark specimen in JPG format, minimum **1000 x 1000 pixels**.
- List of goods/services. The firm drafts the specification using the DJKI-accepted terms list.
- Statement of ownership signed by the applicant.
- For priority claims: a copy of the priority document, which must be submitted within **3 months** of the Indonesian filing date.

## 3. Internal Timeline
- Instructions received to filing: **2 working days** for standard filings, **same day** for urgent filings (urgent surcharge **IDR 1,500,000** per application).
- Filing receipt reported to the client within **1 working day** after filing.

## 4. Official Process (general overview)
After filing, DJKI checks formalities, publishes the application for **2 months** for opposition, then carries out substantive examination. A registered trademark is protected for **10 years** from the filing date and can be renewed.

## 5. Professional Fees (per class)
- Standard filing: **IDR 3,500,000** (official fees charged separately at cost).
- Multi-class discount: **10%** off professional fees from the third class onwards.
