# SOP-TM-03 - Responding to Substantive Refusals (Office Actions)
## 1. Purpose
How the firm handles a provisional refusal issued by the DJKI examiner during substantive examination.

## 2. Deadlines
- The official deadline to respond to a provisional refusal is **30 days** from the date of the notice.
- Internal rule: the client must be reported to within **2 working days** of receipt, and the draft response must be ready **7 calendar days** before the official deadline.
- Every deadline is entered in the docketing system with reminders at **14, 7 and 3 days** before the due date.

## 3. Response Strategy
1. Analyse the cited prior marks and the examiner's reasoning.
2. Choose one or more arguments: dissimilarity of marks, dissimilarity of goods, co-existence evidence, letter of consent from the prior owner, or amendment/limitation of the specification.
3. A senior associate approves the strategy before the client is asked for instructions.

## 4. Fees
- Response to provisional refusal: **IDR 4,000,000** per application.
- Obtaining a letter of consent from a third party: billed hourly (see POL-BILL-01).

## 5. If the Refusal Becomes Final
The client is informed within **1 working day** and advised on appeal to the Trademark Appeal Commission. The associate records the outcome in the matter database with status code **TM-REF-FINAL**.
