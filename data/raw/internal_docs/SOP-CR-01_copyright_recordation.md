# SOP-CR-01 - Copyright Recordation
## 1. Overview
Copyright protection arises automatically, but recordation with DJKI gives the client official evidence of ownership.

## 2. Documents
- Sample of the work (PDF, image, audio or video file).
- Statement of ownership signed by the creator or owner.
- If the owner is a company: a copy of the deed of establishment and the assignment from the creator to the company.

## 3. Timeline and Fees
- Online recordation is filed within **2 working days** of complete documents.
- Professional fee: **IDR 1,500,000** per work.
- Software recordation including source-code excerpt review: **IDR 2,500,000** per work.

## 4. Licensing
Copyright licence agreements prepared by the firm must be recorded with DJKI so that they are enforceable against third parties. Licence drafting is billed hourly under POL-BILL-01.
