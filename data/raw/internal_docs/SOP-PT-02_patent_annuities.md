# SOP-PT-02 - Patent Annuity Payments
## 1. Principle
Annual fees (annuities) must be paid every year to keep a granted patent in force. Non-payment can lead to the patent being deemed lapsed.

## 2. Reminder Schedule
Clients receive annuity reminders **3 months**, **1 month** and **2 weeks** before the due date.

## 3. Payment Rules
- The firm only pays an annuity after receiving **written instructions** from the client (email is accepted).
- Funds must be received before payment unless the client has a signed **standing credit arrangement** approved by the Finance Manager.
- Proof of payment is reported within **2 working days** after payment.

## 4. Fees
- Annuity handling fee: **IDR 600,000** per patent per year.
- Bulk portfolio clients (more than **50 patents**) receive a **20%** discount on handling fees.

## 5. Abandonment
If a client instructs the firm to abandon a patent, the instruction must be confirmed in writing and recorded with status **PT-ABANDONED**.
