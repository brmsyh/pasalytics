# HR-02 - Onboarding and Training for New Staff
## 1. Onboarding Programme
New associates and paralegals follow a **4-week** onboarding programme:
- Week 1: firm policies, conflict checks, confidentiality and the document management system.
- Week 2: trademark practice (search, filing, office actions, oppositions).
- Week 3: patents, industrial designs and copyright.
- Week 4: docketing, billing and client communication, ending with a written assessment.

## 2. Mentoring
Each new joiner is assigned a mentor (a senior associate) for the first **6 months**.

## 3. Continuing Training
- All fee earners must complete at least **20 hours** of internal or external training per year.
- Monthly internal product-knowledge sessions are held on the **last Friday of each month**.

## 4. Probation
The probation period is **3 months**. Confirmation requires a satisfactory assessment and mentor feedback.
