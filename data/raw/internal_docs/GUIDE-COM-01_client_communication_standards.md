# GUIDE-COM-01 - Client Communication Standards
## 1. Response Time Commitments
- Acknowledge every client email within **4 working hours**.
- Provide a substantive answer to routine questions within **2 working days**.
- Urgent matters (deadline within 5 working days) receive a substantive answer the **same working day**.

## 2. Reporting Official Documents
Clients are informed of every official document (filing receipt, publication, office action, certificate) within **2 working days** of receipt.

## 3. Language
Indonesian clients are served in Bahasa Indonesia unless they request English. Foreign clients and foreign firms are served in English.

## 4. Tone and Content
- Every report states the next action and the deadline, if any, in the first paragraph.
- Avoid legal jargon with non-lawyer clients; explain terms such as "opposition" and "office action" in plain language.

## 5. Escalation
Client complaints are escalated to the responsible partner within **1 working day** and logged in the Client Feedback Register.
