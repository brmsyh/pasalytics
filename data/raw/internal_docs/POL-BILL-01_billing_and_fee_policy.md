# POL-BILL-01 - Billing and Fee Policy
## 1. Fee Structures
- **Fixed fees** apply to standard filings, renewals and responses listed in the practice SOPs.
- **Hourly rates** apply to advisory, litigation support, licensing and any work not covered by a fixed fee:
  - Partner: **IDR 3,500,000 per hour**
  - Senior associate: **IDR 2,200,000 per hour**
  - Associate: **IDR 1,500,000 per hour**
  - Paralegal: **IDR 600,000 per hour**
- Time is recorded in **6-minute units** (0.1 hour).

## 2. Disbursements
Official fees, translation, courier and legalisation costs are passed through at cost. Bank charges for foreign transfers are borne by the client.

## 3. Estimates
Any advisory matter expected to exceed **IDR 25,000,000** requires a written cost estimate approved by the client before work begins. If actual costs are likely to exceed the estimate by more than **15%**, the client must be informed before continuing.

## 4. Invoicing
Invoices are issued at the end of each month or upon completion of a fixed-fee task. Foreign clients are invoiced in **USD** at the exchange rate on the invoice date; Indonesian clients are invoiced in IDR.
