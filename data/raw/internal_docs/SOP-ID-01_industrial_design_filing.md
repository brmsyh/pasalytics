# SOP-ID-01 - Industrial Design Filing
## 1. Overview
A registered industrial design in Indonesia is protected for **10 years** from the filing date and cannot be renewed.

## 2. Drawings and Images
- Drawings or photographs must show at least these views: front, back, left, right, top, bottom and perspective.
- Images must be in black and white line drawings or clear photographs with a plain background, in JPG format.
- The firm's design team checks every view set for consistency before filing.

## 3. Novelty Warning
Associates must ask the client whether the design has been disclosed publicly. The client must be warned that prior public disclosure can destroy novelty, subject to the limited grace period under the law.

## 4. Timeline and Fees
- Filing within **3 working days** of complete instructions.
- Professional fee: **IDR 3,000,000** per design.
- Multiple designs in one application (same class, same set): **IDR 1,000,000** for each additional design.
