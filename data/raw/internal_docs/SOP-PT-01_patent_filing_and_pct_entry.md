# SOP-PT-01 - Patent Filing and PCT National Phase Entry
## 1. Types of Protection (overview)
- Patent: protection for **20 years** from the filing date.
- Simple patent: protection for **10 years** from the filing date.

## 2. PCT National Phase Entry
- The national phase entry deadline for Indonesia is **31 months** from the priority date.
- Internal rule: complete instructions and the full specification must be received at least **1 month** before the deadline. Instructions received later are charged an urgent surcharge of **IDR 2,500,000**.
- Translation into Indonesian is handled by the firm's approved technical translators. Standard turnaround: **10 working days** for up to 30,000 words.

## 3. Request for Substantive Examination
The firm diarises the deadline for the request for substantive examination and sends client reminders **3 months** and **1 month** before it falls due.

## 4. Fees
- PCT national phase entry professional fee: **IDR 8,000,000**.
- Translation: **IDR 350 per word**.
- Request for substantive examination: **IDR 3,000,000** professional fee.
