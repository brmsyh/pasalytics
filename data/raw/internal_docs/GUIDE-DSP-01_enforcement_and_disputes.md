# GUIDE-DSP-01 - IP Enforcement and Dispute Escalation
## 1. Scope
Cease-and-desist letters, cancellation actions, online marketplace takedowns and support for litigation in the Commercial Court.

## 2. Intake
- Any client report of infringement must be assessed within **3 working days**.
- The associate prepares an Enforcement Assessment Memo covering ownership, evidence of infringement, available remedies and estimated costs.

## 3. Cease-and-Desist Letters
- Fixed professional fee: **IDR 5,000,000** per letter.
- The standard response period given to the infringer is **14 days**.

## 4. Marketplace Takedowns
- Takedown requests on major e-commerce platforms are filed within **2 working days** of client approval.
- Fee: **IDR 400,000** per listing, with a minimum of **IDR 2,000,000** per batch.

## 5. Escalation to Litigation
Before recommending court action, the matter must be reviewed by the **Head of Disputes**, and the client must approve a written litigation budget. Litigation work is billed hourly under POL-BILL-01.
