# HR-01 - Working Hours and Leave Policy
## 1. Working Hours
- Core office hours are **08:30 to 17:30 WIB**, Monday to Friday.
- Staff may work from home up to **2 days per week** with their team leader's approval, provided deadline coverage is maintained.

## 2. Annual Leave
- Permanent staff receive **14 working days** of annual leave per year.
- Leave requests of up to 3 days must be submitted at least **5 working days** in advance; longer leave needs **14 days** notice.
- Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over to the next year and must be used before 31 March.

## 3. Sick Leave
Sick leave of more than **2 consecutive days** requires a doctor's note.

## 4. Deadline Coverage
Before going on leave, staff must hand over all deadlines falling during their absence to a named colleague and record the handover in the docketing system.
