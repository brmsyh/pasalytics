# POL-KYC-01 - Client Due Diligence and Power of Attorney
## 1. Client Due Diligence
- Indonesian companies: copy of deed of establishment, latest amendment, NIB (business identification number) and director's ID.
- Foreign companies: certificate of incorporation or equivalent extract, and the name and position of the signatory.
- Individuals: ID card (KTP) or passport.
Due diligence documents are refreshed every **3 years** for active clients.

## 2. Power of Attorney (POA)
- A POA must name the firm's registered IP consultants as attorneys.
- POAs are signed by an authorised signatory of the client. Wet-ink originals are not required for filing; a scanned copy is accepted under firm policy.
- A general POA covering all IP matters is valid until revoked. A specific POA covers only the matter named in it.

## 3. Storage
Client due diligence documents are stored in the restricted KYC folder and are accessible only to the Risk and Compliance team and the responsible partner.
