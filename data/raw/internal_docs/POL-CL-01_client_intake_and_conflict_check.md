# POL-CL-01 - Client Intake and Conflict Check Policy
## 1. Purpose
No new matter may be opened until a conflict check is cleared.

## 2. Conflict Check Procedure
1. The intake coordinator enters the client name, the counterparty names and the marks/patents involved in the conflict system.
2. The system checks existing clients, adverse parties and the firm's watch database.
3. The result must be cleared by the Risk and Compliance Officer within **1 working day**.
4. If a potential conflict is found, the matter is escalated to the **Managing Partner**, who decides whether to decline or to seek informed written consent from both clients.

## 3. Matter Opening
- Each new matter receives a matter number in the format **LIP-YYYY-NNNNN** (for example LIP-2026-00412).
- An engagement letter must be signed before substantive work starts, except for urgent deadline matters, where the engagement letter must be signed within **5 working days**.

## 4. Declined Matters
Declined matters are logged with the reason. The firm does not disclose the identity of other clients when declining.
